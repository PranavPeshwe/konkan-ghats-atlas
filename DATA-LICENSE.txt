Field-guide additions:
data/maharashtra-map-names.json and data/maharashtra-name-cache/ retain
identity-matched OSM native-language name tags, retrieval dates and object
versions from a finite map-label sample. They are OSM-derived data under
the ODbL terms above. data/maharashtra-label-samples.json records sampled
viewports at zooms 5-16, not an exhaustive state gazetteer. Viewport edges
can include adjacent states; no administrative boundary is inferred.
Contributor account metadata is not retained. Generated fallback spellings
are marked approximate and are not represented as official names.

data/field-guide.json includes OSRM-derived stage metrics and road-section
geometry under the OSM database terms above, plus NASA/USGS SRTMGL1 v003
terrain samples obtained from the Open Topo Data public service.
https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/catalog/lpcloud-srtmgl1-003
https://www.opentopodata.org/datasets/srtm/
The SRTM samples are US-government terrain data, not a road-deck survey.
Dataset attribution, source coordinates, interpolation method, retrieval
timestamps and original response values are retained. The API server
software itself is not bundled.

Measurement audit:
data/ghat-measurement-audit.json adds separately retained ASTER GDEM v3
terrain values (NASA/METI, supplied through Open Topo Data) and Copernicus
DEM 2021 GLO-90 values (Copernicus programme / ESA, supplied through
Open-Meteo). Dataset acknowledgement: DOI 10.5270/ESA-c5d3d65.
https://www.opentopodata.org/datasets/aster/
https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/elevation-api
Source values, coordinates, request URLs, timestamps and API responses are
retained separately. No averaging or substitution creates a surveyed road
height. Product disagreement is not a statistical uncertainty interval.

data/bor-road-options.json contains OSM/OSRM-derived expressway itineraries
under the OSM database terms above. data/bor-route-cache/ retains bounded
OpenStreetMap motorway and tunnel-way evidence from Overpass. Two routed
prefixes and complete directed coastal legs are retained in data/cache/.
Official MSRDC/MoEFCC and Maharashtra Highway Police documents are linked
for project identity and dated regulation, not reproduced in full.

The atlas's legacy lengthKm and entry/exit anchor fields describe selected
routing intervals, not independently established complete-ghat boundaries.
Historical, approximate ghat-only and project-road references have separate
scope labels in data/ghat-length-sources.json. Unestablished current exact
whole-ghat lengths remain null in the audit. Reference documents are linked,
not redistributed in full.

data/ghat-stories.json contains original short factual paraphrases and
explicitly attributed traditions. Gazetteers and government publications
retain their own rights. No inference of a guard-fort role, name origin,
or historical certainty is licensed by a mere proximity or local story.

This is a researched snapshot, not live navigation, legal access advice,
traffic, weather, closure or road-condition information. Road
availability and restrictions must be checked independently before travel.
